**Principal AI Engineer | Content Systems Architect | Enterprise Adoption Strategist**

I build systems that make people more capable — not more replaceable. From enterprise Claude Skills adoption playbooks to AI-powered content engines generating millions of impressions, I design the infrastructure that turns AI from a novelty into a daily driver for entire organizations.

Most of my work lives in private repos — enterprise MCP servers, production Claude Skills, internal automation systems, and proprietary content engines. What you'll find here are my public tools, experiments, and contributions to the open-source AI ecosystem.

I also lead a local AI education community in St. Petersburg, FL — teaching career changers and developers how to thrive in the agentic era.
